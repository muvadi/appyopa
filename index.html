appYOPA: Your Hub for Paid Surveys and Online Tasks

appYOPA is a comprehensive management and information hub designed to centralize and organize online earning activities, such as paid surveys, micro-tasks, gaming, and product testing [1, 2]. Rather than acting as a direct payment provider, it serves as a "command center" or personal logbook to help users optimize their time and track progress across various third-party platforms [3, 4].

Key Functionalities

  • Centralized Panel Database: The app indexes major global and local survey platforms (such as LifePoints, GFK, and Toluna), cataloging them by parameters like minimum age, difficulty level, and supported languages [5, 6].
  • Advanced Filtering: Users can find the most suitable panels by filtering results in real-time by activity type, payout thresholds (e.g., 0€-5€), and preferred payment methods like PayPal or Amazon Gift Cards [5, 7, 8].
  • Personalized Tracking: Organize your workflow by marking platforms as "I'm registered", "Keep an eye on", or by using the "dislike" function to hide panels you aren't interested in [5, 9, 10].
  • Earnings Registry & Currency Converter: You can record rewards received from each platform. For users working across different regions, an integrated currency converter connects to real-time exchange rates to provide a clear overview of total earnings [10, 11].
  • Integrated Personal Notes: Use a dedicated space inside each platform card to write down reminders, progress, or useful codes [9, 12].
  • Customized Experience: If a favorite panel is missing, you can manually add new cards by entering the name, direct link, and description yourself [6, 9, 11].

Privacy and Availability

appYOPA is completely free and respects your privacy [13]. It requires no registration because it does not use a server-side database; instead, all your data is stored exclusively in your device's Local Storage [12, 13].

You can access appYOPA in two ways:

  • Web App: Available at appyopa.com [10].
  • Android App: Available on the Google Play Store [3, 14].
