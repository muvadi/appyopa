appYOPA: Your Hub for Paid Surveys and Online Tasks

appYOPA is a comprehensive management and information hub designed to centralize and organize online earning activities, such as paid surveys, micro-tasks, gaming, and product testing. Rather than acting as a direct payment provider, it serves as a "command center" or logbook to help users optimize their time and track their progress across various third-party platforms.

Key Functionalities

Centralized Panel Database: The app indexes major global and local survey platforms (such as LifePoints, GFK, and Toluna), cataloging them by minimum age, difficulty level, and supported languages.

Advanced Filtering: Users can search for opportunities in real-time based on specific criteria, such as payout thresholds (e.g., 0€-5€), preferred payment methods (PayPal, Amazon, Zalando), and the type of activity.

Personalized Tracking: You can organize your workflow by marking platforms as "I'm registered" (I miei panel), "Keep an eye on" (Tengo d'occhio), or using the "dislike" function to hide panels you are not interested in.

Earnings Registry & Currency Converter: A dedicated section allows you to record received rewards (cash, vouchers, etc.). For users working across different regions, an integrated currency converter connects to real-time exchange rates to provide a clear overview of total earnings.

Manual Entry & Notes: If a platform is missing from the database, you can manually add it yourself. Additionally, you can save personal notes on each platform card to track progress, reminders, or useful codes.

Privacy-Centric Design: appYOPA is completely free and requires no registration. It does not use a server-side database; instead, all your data is stored exclusively in your device's Local Storage, ensuring 100% privacy.
